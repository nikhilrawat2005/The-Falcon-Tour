Japan Day-by-Day Itinerary

Walk the towering snow corridors of the Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route, from the temples of Nagano to the neon of Osaka.

01
Arrival in Tokyo

Welcome to the Neon Metropolis

Arrive at Tokyo Narita or Haneda Airport. Take a private transfer to your Shinjuku hotel. In the evening, walk the neon-lit alleyways of Shinjuku and cross the famous Shibuya scramble crossing.

Airport Meet & Greet Shibuya Crossing Walk Shinjuku Neon Tour
02
Tokyo Exploration

Ancient Shrines & Modern Pop Culture

Visit Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa with its Nakamise shopping street, then Meiji Jingu shrine and the Harajuku Takeshita Street. Pass the Imperial Palace East Gardens for a view of the grounds.

Asakusa Senso-ji Temple Meiji Jingu Shrine Harajuku Takeshita Street
03
Mount Fuji & Lake Kawaguchi

Views of the Sacred Peak

Take a scenic drive towards Mount Fuji. Visit the Oshino Hakkai spring village and viewpoints along Lake Kawaguchi. Weather permitting, admire Fuji from Arakurayama Sengen Park and the Chureito Pagoda.

Lake Kawaguchi Views Oshino Hakkai Village Chureito Pagoda
04
Tokyo to Nagano (Shinkansen)

Temple City & Zenko-ji

Board the Shinkansen bullet train to Nagano. Visit the historic Zenko-ji Temple, stroll the Nakamise shopping street, and enjoy an evening at leisure with a local soba dinner.

Shinkansen Bullet Train Zenko-ji Temple Nakamise Shopping Street
05
Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route

The Famous Snow Corridor

Cross the Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route in a full-day. Transfer to Ogizawa, ride the trolley bus over Kurobe Dam, and walk the towering snow corridor walls at Murodo - up to 15-20 metres from April to June. Cable-car down to Tateyama and continue to Toyama for the overnight stay.

Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route Murodo Snow Corridor Walk Kurobe Dam
06
Toyama to Nagoya

Alpine City to Castle City

See a little of Toyama in the morning - Toyama Castle ruins park and the canal promenade. Take the Shinkansen to Nagoya and visit Nagoya Castle grounds in the evening.

Toyama Castle Park Shinkansen to Nagoya Nagoya Castle Grounds
07
Nagoya to Kyoto

Samurai & Old Town Lanterns

Visit Atsuta Jingu shrine and the Osu shopping arcade in the morning. Travel by train to Kyoto in the afternoon and take an evening lantern walk through the Gion district.

Atsuta Jingu Shrine Osu Shopping Arcade Gion Evening Walk
08
Kyoto Highlights

Golden Pavilions & Torii Gates

See the golden Kinkaku-ji pavilion, wander the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, and hike the endless red torii gates at Fushimi Inari shrine.

Kinkaku-ji Golden Pavilion Arashiyama Bamboo Grove Fushimi Inari Torii Gates
09
Nara & Osaka

Deer, Buddhas & Neon

Travel to Nara in the morning to meet the bowing sika deer of Nara Park and visit the giant Buddha at Todai-ji. Transfer to Osaka in the afternoon and finish the day with a Dotonbori street-food walk.

Nara Park Sika Deer Todai-ji Great Buddha Dotonbori Street Food
10
Osaka & Departure

Castle Farewell

Visit Osaka Castle in the morning, breakfast at Kuromon Market, and shop the Shinsaibashi arcade. Take a private transfer to Kansai International Airport (KIX) for your return flight.

Osaka Castle Grounds Kuromon Market Airport Departure Transfer
