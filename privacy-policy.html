Privacy Policy

Effective from 9 September 2026

Welcome to The Falcon Tour. We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how The Falcon Tour collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, communicate with us, or use our travel-related services.

By using our website and services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

01 / Information We Collect

Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you contact us, submit a travel enquiry, request a quotation, make a booking, or otherwise communicate with us.

This information may include:

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, telephone/mobile number, and other contact details.
  • Travel Information: Destination preferences, travel dates, number of travellers, accommodation preferences, transportation requirements, and itinerary preferences.
  • Traveller Information: Information required to arrange travel services, such as traveller names and other booking-related information.
  • Booking Information: Details relating to your travel enquiry, quotation, reservation, booking, cancellation, or modification.
  • Payment Information: Payment-related information necessary to process your booking or payment. Where payments are processed through third-party payment providers, your payment information may be handled directly by those providers in accordance with their own privacy policies.
  • Communication Information: Records of communications and interactions with our team, including enquiries, requests, feedback, and customer-support communications.
  • Information You Provide: Any other information you voluntarily provide to us while requesting or using our services.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit or use our website, certain information may be collected automatically, depending on the website's technical configuration and the technologies we use.

This may include:

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, and related technical information.
  • Usage Information: Pages visited, time spent on the website, referring pages, and interactions with website features.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: We may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand website usage, and support analytics or marketing activities.

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

02 / How We Use Your Information

How We Use Your Information

We may use your information for the following purposes:

2.1 Providing Travel Services

To provide travel planning, consultation, quotation, booking, reservation, itinerary preparation, transportation, accommodation, and other travel-related services requested by you.

2.2 Processing Enquiries and Bookings

To communicate with you regarding your travel enquiry, quotation, booking, payment, cancellation, modification, or other service-related request.

2.3 Customer Support

To respond to your questions, provide assistance, resolve issues, and communicate important information relating to your travel arrangements.

2.4 Personalisation

To understand your travel requirements and preferences and provide suitable destinations, itineraries, accommodation, and travel options.

2.5 Marketing and Communication

Where permitted by applicable law and, where required, based on your consent, we may use your contact information to send information about travel packages, offers, promotions, destinations, group tours, and other services offered by The Falcon Tour. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by contacting us or using the applicable unsubscribe or opt-out mechanism provided in the communication.

2.6 Website Improvement and Analytics

To understand how visitors use our website, improve its functionality and content, and enhance our products and services.

2.7 Legal and Regulatory Compliance

To comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal obligations, court orders, government requirements, and other lawful requests.

2.8 Protection of Rights and Security

To detect, prevent, investigate, or address fraud, misuse, security incidents, unauthorised activities, or violations of our terms and policies, and to protect the rights, property, and safety of The Falcon Tour, our customers, and others.

03 / How We Share Your Information

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information as part of our ordinary business operations.

We may share relevant information with trusted third parties when reasonably necessary to provide the services you request or for legitimate business, legal, or operational purposes.

3.1 Travel Service Providers

We may share necessary traveller and booking information with hotels, airlines, transportation providers, destination management companies, tour operators, activity providers, visa-related service providers, insurance providers, and other travel suppliers involved in arranging your trip.

Only information reasonably necessary for providing the relevant service should be shared.

3.2 Payment and Technology Service Providers

Where applicable, we may share information with payment processors, website hosting providers, CRM providers, communication platforms, analytics providers, and other technology or operational service providers that assist us in running our business.

3.3 Business Partners

Where necessary for arranging your requested travel services, we may share relevant information with business partners and suppliers working with us to fulfil your travel requirements.

3.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information where required or permitted by applicable law, regulation, legal process, court order, or government authority.

3.5 Protection of Rights and Safety

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to protect our legal rights, customers, employees, business, property, or the safety and security of any person.

04 / International Transfer of Information

International Transfer of Information

Because travel services may involve hotels, airlines, destination management companies, tour operators, and other suppliers located outside India, certain personal information may need to be shared with service providers in other countries.

Where such transfers are necessary to provide the travel services requested by you, we will take reasonable steps consistent with applicable law to handle your information appropriately.

05 / Your Rights and Choices

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information. These may include:

5.1 Access and Correction

You may contact us to request access to or correction of personal information that we hold about you, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification requirements.

5.2 Withdrawal of Consent

Where we process your personal information based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent, subject to applicable law. Withdrawal of consent may affect our ability to provide certain services where the information is necessary for those services.

5.3 Marketing Opt-Out

You may request to stop receiving promotional or marketing communications from us at any time. However, you may continue to receive essential communications relating to an existing enquiry, booking, payment, or travel arrangement.

5.4 Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.
